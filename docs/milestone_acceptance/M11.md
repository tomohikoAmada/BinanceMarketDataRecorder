## Delivered scope

- Additive Catalog capacity samples, per-scope alert state/transitions and
  idempotent operational events; no market-event body enters SQLite.
- Exact integer WARNING/CRITICAL/EMERGENCY thresholds for internal and each
  registered external target, with `LOW_SPACE` plus explicit severity.
- Robust consecutive-slope median rates for 1 h, 6 h, 24 h and 7 d windows,
  80% coverage gates and conservative maximum-available-window selection.
- UTC ETAs for 40%, 15%, emergency and exhaustion with exact `REACHED`,
  `INSUFFICIENT_DATA`, `NOT_APPROACHING` and out-of-range semantics; no
  NaN/infinity output.
- One-minute idempotent internal sampling from the existing runtime-metrics
  boundary, plus internal/per-accessible-target sampling through structured
  `storage forecast`.
- Backlog bytes and oldest-unarchived timestamp/age derived from current chunk
  lifecycle; verified archive release changes the subsequent history.
- Emergency coordinator that suspends non-core work and prioritizes only
  verified archive/delete at EMERGENCY. At the hard reserve it seals active Raw,
  stops Collectors, records `DISK_EMERGENCY_STOP`, and opens a gap.
- `ArchiveManager.release_verified_once()` provides an emergency-safe release
  path that cannot select a merely sealed or unverified source.

## Known limitations

Internal sampling occurs when the running metrics boundary is exercised or when
the forecast CLI is invoked; persistent wall-clock scheduling is not assembled
until the supervised service/LaunchAgent milestones. External targets are
sampled by `storage forecast` and future storage-monitor calls, not while absent.
The forecast intentionally measures net filesystem free-space change, so other
processes on the same filesystem influence exhaustion ETA. The hard-stop
coordinator is implemented and tested with a real Raw spool, but deliberately
was not triggered against the user's production filesystem.

## Data compatibility and rollback

M11 does not alter EventEnvelope, Raw chunks, archive artifacts, or existing
manifests. It additively stores aggregate capacity samples, alerts and
operational events. Forecast history is derived and may be ignored or rebuilt;
rollback must retain the conservative hard stop and never delete unarchived Raw.
