## Semantic contract

V3 records a structurally valid Catalog OPEN in the existing compact
`catalog_transition.current_open` projection. An intermediate OPEN is causal
state, not a blocker and not a sticky finding. A valid OPEN followed by its
exact COMPLETED lifecycle key is eligible once the reconstructed terminal
state is closed. An OPEN remaining at the terminal observation derives
`unresolved_discontinuity`, makes the final `FAIL`, and is ineligible.

The exact lifecycle identity remains
`(market, symbol, stream, gap_id)`. OPEN cannot disappear without an exact
valid completion. No grace timeout or new Catalog/Raw/manifest field was
introduced.

## Bounded verifier and resume behavior

Runtime `finalize()` performs exactly one terminal `sample()`, derives the
terminal blocker from the observer's bounded rolling Catalog OPEN state, and
does not rescan sample files or perform a second Catalog query. The V3 verifier
streams one sample at a time, reconstructs the terminal Catalog state, derives
the deterministic first-open detail from the sorted lifecycle key, and checks
the final blocker/detail/result/eligibility binding.

Resume restores T0, process/service/boot/deployment identity, hash-chain
position, rolling manifest/raw/reconnect state, and `published_catalog_open`.
Valid OPEN may cross observer interruption and later close; terminal OPEN and
silent disappearance remain fail-closed.

The existing one-time baseline, compact transitions, 300-second sample-start
cadence, BOOTTIME authority, and 600-second maximum evidence gap are unchanged.
