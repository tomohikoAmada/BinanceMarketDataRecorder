rotation, continuous RSS/file-handle/queue behavior, archive-backlog stability

long-running reliability risk. These gates must be completed before any

## Compatibility and rollback

Raw framing and immutable payload policy remain compatible. Spot derived
order-book checkpoints move from algorithm v1 to v2 and must be rebuilt from
Raw. Spot snapshot provenance moves to v2 because exact body and rate-limit
headers are added. Existing immutable Raw remains the rollback source.

Rollback disables Spot readiness if the selected bridge proves wrong, restores
the prior transport/reconstructor, rebuilds derived data under a new version,
and preserves every Raw artifact, manifest, Catalog record and external copy.
