## Implemented scope

- Explicit V5/current schema with independent dispatch; V1–V4 readers preserved.
- Atomic additive `operational_event_sequence`, deterministic historical backfill,
  same-transaction trigger, one-to-one checks, idempotent reopen and rollback.
- Three monotonic cursors with 256-row pages in one SQLite snapshot. Exact
  indexed causal companions do not consume companion cursors; subsequent own
  replay must match the pinned canonical identity/digest. Pending bound 256.
- Bounded online identity/readiness/capacity, live/open lifecycle and reconnect
  authority. The 240-second BOOTTIME budget cancels SQLite/Raw units; unfinished
  work remains unacknowledged. No historical manifest inventory online.
- First stopped full baseline, predecessor terminal continuity for later stages,
  unique online stage target, no post-target ordinary samples or audit-time credit.
- Private exact-byte manifest corpus (two coherent reads, descriptor/path guards,
  no symlinks, exclusive objects, fsync/readback, second sorted membership pass)
  and consistent read-only Catalog backup bound by `quiescence-corpus.json`.
- Exact full terminal integrity against frozen controls and streamed registered
  Raw/archive payloads; immutable 512-record / 1 MiB shards and chained roots.
- Online resume reconstructs cursor/causal/readiness authority from the chain;
  terminal resume verifies the exact corpus and immutable deterministic prefix.
- Independent verifier reconstructs online chain/readiness/cursors, frozen corpus,
  historical ledger, causal references, Raw integrity, shard aggregate and final
  eligibility. Producer PASS summaries are insufficient. A completed audit root
  is published only after separate exact reconstruction.
- Production freeze/controller cancellation with 60-second forward-progress
  evidence, 900-second no-progress watchdog, child process-group termination,
  immutable interruption evidence and no total audit-duration cap.

Ordinary work is O(bounded delta + configured live state + unfinished indexed
backlog), not O(all immutable manifests). Full baseline/terminal work intentionally
scales with the qualified control corpus and Raw/archive bytes, using temporary
disk SQLite indexes rather than a full inventory in RAM.

The claim that the reviewed V5 stage/resume actually executes full Catalog
integrity checks is not supported by that head: `V5AcceptanceObserver.__post_init__`
already replaces the real evaluator callback with `catalog_available`; resume
constructs the same observer. Direct-API new/resume regressions retain and prove
this guard. The mutable CLI setup and absence of real-evaluator scaling coverage
were valid review concerns.

The CLI now injects the bounded callback at evaluator construction for every V5
stage/resume/readiness path. Six schema/mode CLI regressions execute the actual
evaluator and V5 online observer/resume, with only OS/identity probes and cadence
control injected. V5 forbids the legacy integrity check; V4 asserts it remains
selected. The 10,000-history operation-count test uses production readiness,
four consecutive observations including resume, and forbidden full-inventory and
legacy integrity-check hooks. No historical manifest is read/parsed/hashed.

so a subsequent legal archive retirement can invalidate historical evidence.

The generic V5 dispatch also ignores a supplied live target resolver; its

Finalization explicitly retains `historical_control_only=False` for its
