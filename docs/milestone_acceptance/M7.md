## Architecture decisions

- ADR-0012 freezes current routes/methods, sparse/polling semantics, rate-limit
  provenance, funding cadence, no-fill rules and isolation boundary.
- Mark stream `T` is next funding time rather than transaction time.
- Liquidation is an official latest-within-1000-ms snapshot stream, not a claim
  of every liquidation order.
- Repeated REST history may duplicate Raw; later normalization owns explicit
  deterministic deduplication.
- Durable daily side-data metrics remain M8; M7 exposes live counters without
  pre-implementing aggregation.

## Excluded scope and known limitations

M7 does not implement M8 durable daily metrics/reports, normalized Parquet,
general replay, archive/storage-volume operations, launchd, GUI/web, accounts,
credentials, strategies, backtests or trading.

The service assembly is not yet exposed through a long-running launchd process;
side-data settings are a strict programmatic Collector configuration boundary.
Liquidation transport silence is semantically valid and cannot by itself prove
market inactivity; connection failures provide the separate quality signal.
REST provenance is the SDK parsed model rather than unavailable raw HTTP bytes.

## Data compatibility and rollback

M7 does not change `event-envelope.v1`, `raw-chunk.v1`, core Spot/USD-M stream
schemas, M6 order books or Catalog schema. It adds new Raw stream names and
`binance-usdm-side-rest-provenance.v1`; existing readers may ignore unknown
streams until updated.

Rollback disables individual or all side-data kinds and reverts M7 while
preserving already sealed Raw/provenance. Core capture remains unchanged.
