## Implementation boundary

V4 is an explicit schema generation. V1, V2, and V3 readers, predecessor
rules, resume behavior, and V3 sticky intermediate semantics remain separate
and unchanged. New identity, readiness, stage-start, stage-sample, and
stage-final records generated by the acceptance CLI use V4.

The V4 observer stores one global readiness episode in constant space. It
accepts only an exact `<configured-market>:<configured-symbol>_core_not_ready`
reason, keeps the deadline across ProductKey/reason rotation, and does not
require a matching Catalog OPEN gap. Deadline expiry and readiness failure are
sticky. A terminal non-READY readiness sample is independently recorded as
`readiness_not_ready` and makes the final ineligible.

The V4 verifier reconstructs the episode from immutable readiness samples and
BOOTTIME timestamps. It independently checks the sample hash chain, identity,
cadence, predecessor schema, Catalog/Raw/manifest incremental authority,
sticky findings, terminal readiness, and final eligibility. It does not trust
the producer's episode summary or blocker list as the source of episode truth.
Resume restores the V4 episode start, sample-chain position, sticky findings,
identity, and existing Catalog/Raw/manifest continuation. A V3 stage cannot
resume as V4 and a V3 predecessor cannot authorize a V4 stage.

The targeted independent-review correction requires exact equality between
sample readiness blockers and the union of independently reconstructed current
readiness findings plus sticky readiness blockers from earlier samples. It
rejects unsupported blockers on READY and in-deadline recoverable samples, and
rejects omission of a demonstrated deadline blocker. Regression fixtures cover
first READY observations at 899, 900, and 901 seconds, with no earlier
over-deadline sample in the 901-second case. Episode projection, recanonicalized
BOOTTIME monotonicity, and sample-chain pointer tampering are tested separately.
Completed V4 stages with only the producer's soft findings reconstruct as
INCOMPLETE and remain ineligible.

## Offline validation scope

Deterministic fixtures cover the historical short recovery incident,
inclusive 899/900-second boundaries, 901-second deadline failure, first READY
after deadline, ProductKey rotation, terminal NOT_READY, readiness FAILED,
non-recoverable NOT_READY, pre-T0 NOT_READY, V4 resume, V1/V2/V3 compatibility,
mixed-schema rejection, episode/blocker/readiness/BOOTTIME/hash tampering,
Catalog OPEN/COMPLETED inheritance, 300-second cadence, and 600-second
observation-gap authority. Full repository validation is required before this
record is considered independently reviewable:
