## Decision and review questions

V5 deliberately moves exact old-history mutation/loss detection from the next ordinary
sample to the **quiescence-frozen control corpus**: any mutation/loss already present
in that corpus must fail before stage eligibility. The 300-second sample target,
600-second maximum online evidence gap, and ADR-0033 900-second global readiness
recovery deadline are unchanged. This externally meaningful change requires
`m22.9-acceptance-evidence.v5`;
V1/V2/V3/V4 dispatch and evidence stay immutable.

## Targeted P1 correction

**P1-A — corpus authority.** `QUIESCENCE_CORPUS_FREEZE` is the qualification
point. The finalizer creates exact-byte, readback-verified private manifest
snapshots with canonical path/length/SHA mappings, performs two coherent source
reads with descriptor/path stability checks, and freezes a read-only SQLite
backup. `quiescence-corpus.json` binds these controls, identity, high-waters,
table counts, and registered targets. Terminal audit and independent verifier
read the frozen controls. Historical mutation/loss already present in that
frozen corpus blocks eligibility; no perpetual claim is made about original
production paths after audit. Raw/archive payload need not be copied: the
terminal auditor streams exact bytes from registered storage while sanctioned
mutators are stopped. Malicious root rewriting all authorities is outside the
stated threat model.

**P1-B — cross-cursor causality.** One bounded SQLite snapshot freezes all
three high-waters, bounded cursor pages, and exact indexed companion lookups.
A lookup does not consume the companion cursor. A selected row advances only
after its required companion is visible and sufficient. A later cursor pass
must match the previously bound primary identity, row digest, transaction/
chunk identity and state. Archive lifecycle lookup uses at most five exact
event and five paired chunk-transition keys per transaction; each key has
cardinality one. The other lookup exception is
the exact indexed discontinuity pair (one STARTED and one COMPLETED); an extra
row fails closed. The ADR includes a `LOCAL_DELETE_PENDING` atomic transaction
example where chunk transition 901 is consumed while archive event 600 is
only causally referenced and its cursor remains behind.

**P2 — terminal full Raw scaling.** The audit may remain O(total qualified Raw
bytes) outside the online window. Implementation qualification must measure
baseline/terminal throughput, Raw and archive bytes per second, peak RSS, and
total duration at the current production corpus size. The integrity gate is
unchanged; audit time earns no Formal credit.
