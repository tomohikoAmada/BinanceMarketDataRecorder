## Architecture decisions

ADR-0018 makes blue/green a whole-market Collector instance handoff rather than
an in-place or per-stream restart. Socket-open health is insufficient.
Candidate promotion requires durable Raw evidence and a synchronized book, then
fresh old/new events after readiness. USD-M side data does not gate core L2.
Raw duplicates remain immutable and identifiable; M15 owns deterministic
normalized deduplication.

If old graceful shutdown times out after confirmed overlap, the ready candidate
is retained and the warning is audited; stopping both would create an avoidable
gap. M13 is a lifecycle library and Catalog protocol only. launchd installation,
process locking, sleep handling, and reboot recovery remain M14.

## Known limitations

M13 does not provide an operator deployment CLI or install a persistent
supervisor. The caller supplies active/candidate Collector objects and manages
which market to deploy. Spot and USD-M are deliberately independent operations;
there is no atomic cross-market release. Candidate readiness covers core L2,
aggTrade, and bookTicker but not failure-isolated USD-M side data.

Catalog audit is durable, but automatic recovery of an interrupted supervisor
process and operating-system process ownership arrive with M14. M13 cannot
guarantee continuity across network, host, power, or simultaneous instance
failure; such intervals must be marked and are exercised in M17. Normalized
overlap deduplication remains M15.

## Data compatibility and rollback

M13 does not change EventEnvelope schema, Raw framing, exact payload bytes,
chunk manifests, archive artifacts, normalized data, or storage paths. Handoff
provenance uses additive `capture_flags`; Catalog additively creates
`deployment_sessions` and `deployment_events`. Older readers that ignore flags
and unknown Catalog tables remain valid.

Before cutover, rollback stops only the candidate and retains old. After
cutover, deploy the prior version as a new candidate with reason `ROLLBACK`
through the same readiness/overlap gate. Preserve Raw overlap, deployment
audit, operational gap evidence, chunks, manifests, archive state, and Catalog
rows.
